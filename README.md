# 🏏 PitchSide Analytics

> Institutional-grade sports trading intelligence platform powered by Match DNA, Player DNA, predictive analytics, real-time market data, and AI-driven decision support.

---

# 📖 Overview

PitchSide Analytics is a next-generation sports intelligence and trading platform that transforms live sporting events into actionable market opportunities.

The platform combines:

- Real-time match analytics
- Sports asset trading
- Player DNA modeling
- Match DNA intelligence
- Predictive AI forecasting
- Portfolio intelligence
- Sentiment analysis
- Research workspaces
- Professional trading infrastructure

PitchSide is designed to provide a Bloomberg Terminal–like experience for sports markets.
